Modifications: metric reprojection and clipping, geometry repair, omission of
small polygons and a legacy non-building import feature, height and road-width
defaults, raster navigation and inferred approaches. See the dataset's
assumptions and per-building heightSource fields.

Version 2 adds sourced road/roof attributes and procedural visual detail.
Roof rise/orientation, inferred roof shapes, facade patterns, roof equipment,
and supplemental park planting are approximations. Generated trees are
separate from surveyed map trees in decorativeTrees and marked procedural.
Navigation and original footprints are preserved.

No Google Maps or Google Earth imagery or geometry is included.
Overture data is used only in the separate research comparison.
